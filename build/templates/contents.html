Probability, Measure and Martingales

B8.1 from σ-algebras to stopping times, written to be read without having taken Part A Integration. Every idea it borrows from that course is explained where it is first needed.

How to read this

Maths is not read in a straight line, so the pages are built for jumping around. Every mention of a numbered result, like Theorem 1.11, is a link: hover over it to read the statement where you are, click to go there, and use the ↩ back button that appears bottom-left to return. On a phone, tap once to preview and again to go.

Numbering follows the notes, and the small page numbers in the margin point into them. Grey Background boxes explain material the notes take from Part A Integration or elsewhere. Dashed Pause boxes hide worked answers to the exercises the notes leave to you. Sidenotes carry asides you can skip.
